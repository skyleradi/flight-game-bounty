Ridge Runner

A valley campaign in a light aeroplane

You have the controls of a single-engine monoplane at the mouth of a mountain valley, and five sorties to fly. Every one of them is a gate course up the valley and back, and every one of them is flown in different weather: still air first, then a crosswind you have to hold off on the strip, then cloud that swallows the course, then a gorge that will not forgive a lazy turn, and finally a storm.

Take off, thread the gates, and put her down on the runway again. Watch the fuel, the airspeed and the clock: the wing only carries you while it is flying, and a heavy touchdown or a belly full of rock costs you the airframe. Each round scores out of a thousand and unlocks the next.
